Our Story

Miyo was born from a simple idea: to build a dessert space where you can create something that’s truly your own, and feel truly at home. A place filled with good energy and happiness, where everyday moments feel a little more joyful, and you leave lighter than you came in.

The intention was to give people the freedom to create, to mix, match, swirl, and create something that’s entirely theirs. With delicious desserts, consistently high quality, and a wide variety of options , Miyo is about everyday joy, shared moments, and little bursts of happiness.

Starting with our first location, we’re building spaces where everyone feels at home, where quality and hospitality go hand in hand, and where good vibes aren’t just an afterthought, they’re the main ingredient.
